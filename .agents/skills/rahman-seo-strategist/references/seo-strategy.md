# Organic SEO strategy

## North-star outcome

Increase qualified, consented consultation demand—not raw sessions—by becoming the most useful verifiable source for plastic-surgery decision support in Islamabad and for relevant international patients considering Pakistan.

Primary conversions: completed consultation request, WhatsApp consultation click, phone click, and confirmed booking. Track qualified consultations and procedures separately from leads.

## Audiences and markets

1. Islamabad/Rawalpindi patients seeking a local surgeon or clinic.
2. Patients elsewhere in Pakistan comparing procedures, recovery, cost factors, and travel to Islamabad.
3. Pakistani diaspora and other international patients evaluating virtual consultation, travel logistics, length of stay, follow-up, and continuity of care.

Do not target cities with a location page unless the clinic is operational and the page offers unique, verifiable local value.

## Site model

Use a hub-and-cluster structure:

- Home: clinic/entity positioning and main service pathways.
- `/procedures`: service hub.
- One substantial canonical page per verified procedure and primary local/commercial intent.
- `/doctors/[slug]`: clinician entities, credentials, experience, and reviewed content.
- `/locations/islamabad`: only if it adds unique NAP, access, facility, parking, hours, and local proof beyond contact.
- `/international-patients`: travel and remote-consultation hub.
- `/case-studies`: consented cases linked to procedure pages, with patient variability and useful context.
- `/news` or preferably `/guides`: educational support content. Do not rename existing URLs without a redirect plan.

## Keyword-to-page rules

- Transactional/local query -> procedure or location page.
- Cost query -> a transparent cost section on the procedure page, supported by a detailed guide only when the guide serves a distinct intent.
- Comparison, preparation, recovery, or question query -> supporting guide linked to the procedure page.
- Surgeon-name or credential query -> clinician profile.
- “Best surgeon” query -> an objective guide to evaluating qualifications and consultation fit; never claim the clinic is best.
- Multiple synonymous queries with the same intent -> one strong page, not separate near-duplicates.

## Initial topic clusters to validate with live data

These are hypotheses, not claims of search volume or difficulty.

### Tummy tuck / abdominoplasty

- Core: tummy tuck Islamabad, abdominoplasty Islamabad/Pakistan, tummy tuck cost Islamabad.
- Support: mini vs full tummy tuck; tummy tuck vs liposuction; recovery week by week; scar development; candidacy after pregnancy or weight loss; diastasis questions; consultation checklist; travel after tummy tuck.

### Liposuction and Lipo 360

- Core: liposuction Islamabad, liposuction cost Islamabad/Pakistan, Lipo 360 Islamabad, Lipo 360 cost Pakistan.

### Mommy makeover

- Core: mommy makeover Islamabad/Pakistan, mommy makeover cost Pakistan.
- Support: procedures commonly combined; timing after pregnancy/breastfeeding; staged vs combined surgery; recovery planning with children; candidacy and risk discussion; travel/aftercare.

- Core: breast reduction Islamabad, breast augmentation Islamabad, verified cost-intent variants.

## Research and prioritization

Maintain a keyword map with one row per cluster, not every word order. Score evidence-backed candidates using:

- 25% conversion intent;
- 20% business fit;
- 15% topical relevance;
- 15% low-competition evidence;
- 10% demand;
- 10% trend direction;
- 5% SERP/content gap.

Use 0–5 inputs and multiply the weighted total by 20. Apply hard gates after scoring: service actually offered, clinic facts verified, medically reviewable, distinct target intent, and no cannibalization. A high numerical score does not override a failed gate.

Prioritize in this order:

1. Existing pages already receiving impressions but with weak CTR, position, intent fit, or conversion.
2. Dedicated pages for verified high-value procedures.
3. Local/entity trust pages and clinician profiles.
4. Bottom- and middle-funnel guides that remove consultation objections.
5. Broader awareness content only when it supports topical authority and has a clear patient purpose.

## Trend workflow

- Compare at least 12 months and, for seasonality, five years when Google Trends data permits.
- Use Pakistan plus meaningful source-country markets; do not assume global demand equals Islamabad opportunity.
- Check whether a rise reflects news, celebrity interest, unsafe fads, or a durable patient need.
- Publish only when the clinic has genuine expertise and the topic connects to an existing service page.
- Refresh a durable cornerstone rather than producing many slight query variations.

## Local SEO

- Fully verify and complete the Google Business Profile with exact NAP, primary/supplementary categories, hours, appointment URL, services, accessibility/parking facts, and original photos.
- Keep NAP consistent across the site, PM&DC/healthcare listings, major local directories, and reputable medical directories.
- Request honest reviews through a neutral, privacy-respecting process; never gate, script, buy, or fabricate them.
- Respond without revealing treatment or confirming patient status.
- Build local authority through legitimate hospital/association profiles, clinician bios, interviews, community education, and relevant editorial coverage—not bulk directory links.

## On-page template for a procedure page

1. Clear H1 naming the procedure and location naturally.
2. Clinically reviewed summary and who the page is for.
3. What the procedure can and cannot address.
4. Candidacy and consultation assessment.
5. Technique/options actually offered by the clinic.
6. Preparation, anesthesia/facility facts when verified.
7. Recovery phases and variability.
8. Risks, limitations, alternatives, and when to seek medical help.
9. Cost factors and what a quote includes; use exact prices only when approved and maintained.
10. Surgeon/reviewer identity and credentials.
11. Consented case evidence if available.
12. Useful FAQs based on real patient questions.
13. Related guides and procedures.
14. Private consultation CTA and medical disclaimer.

## Copy positioning

Prefer: specific, calm, verifiable, consultation-led, realistic, and patient-safety focused.

Replace vague claims such as “world-class care” with facts such as verified qualifications, named facility standards, consultation process, follow-up schedule, and communication options. Explain what happens rather than promising transformation.

## Authority development

- Publish original clinician explanations, anonymized aggregate questions, clinic process photos, and consented case narratives with meaningful clinical context.
- Create reference-worthy resources: recovery planning checklists, consultation question sheets, Pakistan travel-planning guides, and transparent cost-factor explainers.
- Seek earned links from real affiliations, professional profiles, hospitals, universities, local media, and useful collaborations. Reject paid link schemes, private blog networks, mass guest posts, and irrelevant directories.

## Measurement

### Weekly

- Indexing/coverage anomalies, manual actions, security issues, lead-path failures, and ranking changes for priority clusters.

### Monthly

- Non-brand clicks/impressions, local-pack actions, CTR by page/query, priority-cluster visibility, conversions by landing page, qualified lead rate, and top assisted pages.

### Quarterly

- Content decay, cannibalization, link quality, medical-review freshness, procedure/service accuracy, GBP completeness, Core Web Vitals field data, and conversion-to-booking quality.

Do not judge a medical SEO program by traffic alone. Report qualified consultations, booked consultations, cost per qualified organic lead, and landing-page conversion rate alongside visibility.

## Phased roadmap

### Phase 0: launch safety

Verify claims, consent, clinicians, legal text, production URL, robots, sitemap, analytics, Search Console, and indexability.

### Phase 1: foundations

Build dedicated procedure and doctor pages; add canonicals, accurate metadata, article/breadcrumb/entity schema, author/reviewer blocks, real update dates, and deliberate internal links.

### Phase 2: commercial coverage

Publish verified procedure pages and cost/candidacy/recovery sections based on live demand. Improve home, about, contact, and international-patient copy.

### Phase 3: topic clusters

Publish two to four medically reviewed, genuinely useful pieces monthly only if review capacity exists. Refresh winners before increasing volume.

### Phase 4: authority and optimization

Earn relevant coverage/citations, expand original evidence, improve pages from Search Console data, consolidate cannibalizing content, and test consultation UX.
